Guides and checklists for IT infrastructure

Here you will find practical guides to help verify what needs to be prepared or checked in the IT environment. They are intended for those who want to have more order in the infrastructure, better orientation and less improvisation.

Each guide is focused on a specific area and can be used in the design, audit, stabilization or improvement of existing infrastructure.

In practice, they are most useful in cases where you want to quickly check if something important is overlooked or unclearly set.
