Services for companies that want their IT to be more stable, clearer and better prepared for changes

We help small, medium and large companies with areas that are important for the reliable functioning of IT. It is not only about the technologies themselves, but also about them being properly designed and managed in an understandable manner and realistically usable in everyday operation.

Our services are most often used by companies that:

  • have a confusing or unstable IT environment,
  • are preparing a change, migration or modernization,
  • they need to better cover monitoring, logs, backups or the cloud,
  • they want to have more order in IT and lower operational risks.

Simplified: we help companies set up important areas of the infrastructure so that IT supports the operation of the company, rather than hindering it.

What companies most often need to solve

  • lack of insight into how the environment is set up,
  • repeated technical problems and incidents,
  • poor monitoring, logs or backup,
  • confusion when migrating to the cloud or upgrading,
  • dependence on individuals and insufficient documentation.

How we work with clients

  • Audit – we name the risks, weaknesses and quick opportunities for improvement
  • Proposal – we will prepare recommendations, architecture and priorities
  • Stabilization – we will help to implement measures that reduce risk and chaos in operation

Pricing and scope of engagement

We do not publish prices on the website, because the scope of the engagement depends on the size of the environment, available access, documentation status and expected output.

In practice, however, we work with a clearly defined scope and agreed deliverables, to make it clear from the beginning what the goal of the engagement is.
