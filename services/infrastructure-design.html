IT infrastructure design for companies that want to have more order, stability and security in IT

When IT in the company was created gradually without a clear plan, over time it will become an environment, which is difficult to manage, changes badly, and in the case of problems, it is not clear where the real cause is.

We help small, medium and large companies design the infrastructure to be transparent, stable, safe and better prepared for growth, changes and daily operation.

This service is especially suitable when:

  • the company is growing and the original IT solutions are no longer enough,
  • migration to the cloud or modernization of the environment is being prepared,
  • IT is unclear, poorly documented or dependent on one person,
  • recurring incidents, outages or problems that are solved over and over again.

IT infrastructure design is not just about "drawing the architecture". It is about understanding the current situation, naming the risks and proposing a solution, which will work both technically and operationally.

When is this service most useful for companies?

  • before a major change in IT,
  • when growing the company or expanding the team,
  • when services are added and overview is lost,
  • when there is a lack of documentation, monitoring, backup or clear rules of operation.

Even if you are not a technical person, we can explain the current situation clearly, name the risks and suggest what makes sense to address first.

Feel free to contact us

Who is the service suitable for?

  • Small and medium-sized companies, who want to have more reliable and transparent IT
  • Companies with an internal IT team, who lack time or a broader architectural perspective
  • Companies before the change – on-premises infrastructure, migration, cloud, hybrid, modernization or consolidation
  • Owners and management, who want to better understand the state of IT and its risks

What will the company gain?

  • greater order and overview in the infrastructure,
  • clearer decision-making during changes and investments in IT,
  • lower risk of outages and errors during operation and changes,
  • better preparedness for company growth and new projects,
  • a clear plan of what to deal with now and what to do later.

What situations do we most often help with?

Most often, these are companies that already feel that their IT needs a clearer structure and better setup.

IT was created gradually and lacks a clear system

When the environment was expanded piecemeal, it tends to be confusing, with unclear dependencies and high dependency on individuals.

The company is preparing for change or growth

New systems, new teams, on-premises infrastructure, cloud or hybrid environments often require a better design than what has been sufficient so far.

Problems are solved repeatedly, but without removing the cause

When incidents are repeated, the problem is often deeper in the design of the environment, not just one particular tool or server.

What you receive from the infrastructure design

Overview of the current state

We will find out how the environment works today, where there are weaknesses, ambiguities and the main operational risks.

Architecture design and next steps

We will suggest what to modify, what to keep and which changes will have the greatest benefit for stable operation.

Documents for the technical team and management

We prepare deliverables in such a way that they can be used for technical implementation and for deciding on priorities and investments.

If necessary, the output can also contain:

  • architectural design and diagrams,
  • technical recommendations and priorities,
  • operational areas such as monitoring, logs, backup or recovery,
  • naming risks, dependencies and trade-offs.

How we work together

1) We will understand your environment and needs

We will look at the current state, problems, limitations and what is really important for your business.

2) We will propose a reasonable and clear plan

We will prepare recommendations and a proposed solution so that they are practical, usable and make sense both technically and commercially.

3) We will also help with the next steps

We will help with stabilization, the introduction of measures or consultation during the implementation of changes.

Technical areas that we often deal with during design

For technically focused clients, we also cover specific areas of infrastructure and operations.

On-premises, cloud and hybrid environments

Design and arrangement of on-premises infrastructure and environments in AWS, Azure and GCP, including their interconnection.

DDI: DNS / DHCP / IPAM

Basic services on which the reliable functioning of the network and other systems depend.

NTP: time infrastructure

Correct time in systems is important for logs, authentication, certificates and audits.

Monitoring and log management

Setting up monitoring of the environment so that problems are detected early and the team can react faster.

Backup and recovery

We help to set up backups and recovery so that it is not just a formality, but a really usable protection.

Access, segmentation and basic security

Better set-up of access and separation of systems reduces risks and chaos in managing the environment.
