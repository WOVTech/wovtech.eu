We help small, medium and large companies clean up and stabilize IT so that it does not hinder their work or growth

We are a partner for companies that want to have more order, overview and security in their IT. When the infrastructure is unclear, unstable or growing without a clear system, we will help you set it up in an understandable and practical way.

We mainly help with infrastructure design, cloud, monitoring, logs, backup and stabilization of the IT environment.

Typical procedure: analysis → solution design → operation stabilization

Who we work with

  • for small, medium and large companies, who want to have reliable IT,
  • for companies that are growing and the original solutions are no longer enough for them,
  • for companies with an internal IT team that lacks time or architectural insight,
  • for management who need to understand the state of IT and make better decisions based on it.

Even if you are not technically oriented, we can name the problem comprehensibly and propose, which makes sense to address first.

What do we do for companies?

Design and modification of IT infrastructure

We design the IT environment so that it is clear, secure, stable and ready for further development.

On-premises, cloud and hybrid solutions

We help with the design and stabilization of on-premises and cloud environments in AWS, Azure and GCP, including their interconnection.

Monitoring, logs, backups and recovery

We set up important operational areas in such a way that the company can detect problems in time and manage recovery after an incident.

IT support

We help with technical requirements, incidents and system management in on-premises, cloud and hybrid environments.
