Articles and practical guidance on IT infrastructure design and stabilisation

On this page you will find practical articles and recommendations that help companies better understand topics such as monitoring, logs, backups, access, changes and the basic principles of infrastructure design.

We prepare the content so that it is useful:

  • for management that wants to better understand the state of IT,
  • for companies that are preparing for change or modernisation,
  • for internal IT teams who want to verify important areas of operation,
  • for companies that want to better prepare questions before the consultation.

Simplified: articles will help you understand the connections, better name the problem and decide what makes sense to solve.
